BLACK FIRE checkout demo setup

Features added:
- Wishlist page: wishlist.html reads products saved from product cards and persists them in localStorage.
- Three-step checkout: Sign Up / Address / Payment.
- Payment choices: UPI QR and Cash on Delivery only.
- Orange accent color and white backgrounds.
- Cart page links to checkout.html.

IMPORTANT BEFORE REAL USE:
1. UPI QR is a visual placeholder, not a real payment QR. Add your own official QR image as images/upi-qr.png and update checkout.html to display it. Do not use the decorative CSS placeholder to accept payments.
2. Orders are stored only in this browser's localStorage. They are NOT sent to the store or an order-management system.
3. UPI payment is not verified. Confirm payment in your bank/UPI app before dispatching.
4. Connect a secure backend/order service before using checkout with real customers.
5. This is a static frontend demo; no passwords are collected or accounts created.
